Getting there and around

Check your hotel address. Via Aurelia 25, Stagno, 57017 Livorno is in Tuscany, about 300 km north of Rome and 3 hours away by train. This plan assumes you sleep in Rome. If the Livorno booking is right, see the fallback below.

If you are based in Rome

  • From Fiumicino airport: Leonardo Express to Termini, 32 min, €14; or the FL1 regional to Trastevere or Ostiense, €8, if your hotel is on that side.
  • From Ciampino airport: shuttle bus to Termini, about 40 min, €6–7.
  • Public transport: one ATAC ticket covers metro, bus and tram. Single BIT €1.50 for 100 minutes; 24h €8.50; 48h €15; 72h €22; weekly CIS €29. For five days the weekly CIS is the cheapest. Buy at any metro station or tabacchi, or tap a contactless card at the metro gates and on buses (fares are capped daily). Validate paper tickets on every bus.
  • Walking: the centre is compact. The Vatican to the Colosseum is about 4 km; most days here are 5–7 km on foot. Cobbles are hard on feet, so wear your most comfortable shoes.
  • Metro lines you will use: A (red) Ottaviano for the Vatican, Flaminio and Spagna for the north centre, San Giovanni for the Lateran. B (blue) Colosseo, Circo Massimo, Basilica San Paolo. The lines meet at Termini.
  • Where to stay if still choosing: Prati or Borgo for the Vatican, or Monti for the Colosseum. Both have Metro A or B and are walkable to everything.

Fallback: if the hotel really is in Livorno

  • Train: Livorno Centrale to Roma Termini, about 11 direct Intercity trains a day, 2h40–3h20, from about €15–20 each way if booked ahead on Trenitalia. Frecciarossa from Pisa or Florence is faster but from about €64.
  • Hotel to station: Stagno is about 8 km from Livorno Centrale; an LAM Rossa / line 1 bus or a taxi (about €20).
  • Realistic plan: two long day trips rather than four. Take the ~06:00 train, arrive ~09:00, return on the ~19:30–20:30 train. Day trip 1: the Vatican day (General Audience, basilica, dome, Pantheon). Day trip 2: Colosseum, Forum, Capitoline, Santa Maria Maggiore (it is 5 minutes from Termini). Drop the Vatican Museums early slot to 10:00 or skip the Borghese and catacombs.
  • Better: if you can still change it, move the booking to Rome. Four nights in Rome will cost less than four return train days and give you the evenings.

Vatican extras worth a try

  • Scavi tour (St Peter's tomb under the basilica): small groups, roughly €20 pp, requested on the official reservation page. Confirmations take six weeks or more, so a request today is a long shot, but it costs nothing to ask.
  • Sunday Mass times at St Peter's: see the basilica hours page.

First-timer tips

  • Church dress code: shoulders and knees covered for both of you, enforced at St Peter's and the Vatican Museums. A light scarf solves it.
  • Mid-October weather: highs 18–22°C, lows 10–13°C, mostly dry, sunset about 18:30. A light jacket for evenings and the catacombs.
  • Water: the little street fountains (nasoni) run free, cold drinking water. Carry a bottle.
  • Pickpockets: busy on bus 64, Metro A and at Termini. Bag in front, nothing in back pockets.
  • Skip the Roma Pass: at €38–63 it does not pay back against the official prices here.
  • Ignore the touts around the Colosseum and Vatican selling "skip the line"; you already have timed tickets.
  • Eat where locals eat: Trastevere and Monti in the evening, Testaccio for the real Roman kitchen. Cacio e pepe, carbonara, supplì, and the Jewish artichokes in the Ghetto.
  • Coffee: standing at the bar costs about €1.20; sitting down doubles it. Cappuccino is a morning drink.
  • Sundays: Vatican Museums closed; many churches limit sightseeing during Mass.
  • Apps: Google Maps handles ATAC buses well. Moovit shows live bus arrivals.
